# Homemade Falafel

## Ingredients
- 225 g dried chickpeas (about 1 generous cup), soaked overnight
- 55 g mixed fresh herbs (cilantro, parsley, and/or mint leaves; about 2 packed cups)
- 6 scallions, white and pale green parts only, sliced (about 55 g)
- 2 medium garlic cloves, minced (about 2 tsp)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to season after frying
- Vegetable oil, for frying (enough for about a 2 cm / 3/4-inch depth in your pan; typically 500–750 ml)
- Tahini sauce, hummus, and/or zhug, to serve (optional)
- Optional for serving: pita, tomatoes, cucumber, pickles, shredded cabbage

## Method
Before cooking: Rinse chickpeas, cover with plenty of cold water (they should at least triple in volume), and soak at room temperature overnight. The next day, drain, rinse, and dry very well (a salad spinner helps).

00:00 - Add soaked chickpeas, herbs, scallions, garlic, cumin, coriander, and salt to a food processor. Pulse, scraping down as needed, until very finely minced; when squeezed, a handful should just barely hold together.  
00:05 - Scrape mixture into a bowl, cover, and refrigerate for 15 minutes.  
00:20 - Shape into balls: scoop heaping tablespoonfuls and gently compress into rough balls (it won’t roll smoothly like dough). Place on a plate.  
00:30 - Pour oil into a deep skillet or Dutch oven to a depth of about 2 cm / 3/4 inch. Heat to 190°C / 375°F.  
00:40 - Fry in batches, lowering balls in one at a time. Keep oil between 175–190°C / 350–375°F. Cook undisturbed until browned on the bottom, then flip and brown the other side, about 4 minutes total.  
00:45 - Drain on paper towels and season immediately with a pinch of salt. Repeat with remaining falafel.  
00:55 - Serve hot with tahini sauce, hummus, and/or zhug; optionally stuff into pita with tomatoes, cucumber, pickles, and shredded cabbage.
