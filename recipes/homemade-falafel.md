# Homemade Falafel (Makes ~12–15)

## Ingredients
- 225g dried chickpeas (about 1 generous cup / 1/2 lb), soaked
- 55g mixed fresh cilantro/parsley/mint leaves (about 2 cups)
- 55g scallions, white and pale green parts only, sliced (about 6)
- 10ml (2 medium cloves) garlic, minced (about 2 tsp)
- 4g ground cumin (about 1 tsp)
- 2g ground coriander seed (about 1/2 tsp)
- 10g kosher salt (about 2 tsp), plus more for seasoning
- 2 to 3 cups (480 to 720ml) vegetable oil, for frying
- Tahini sauce and/or hummus (and/or zhug) for serving

## Method
Before cooking: Rinse chickpeas, place in a large bowl, and cover with cold water so they can at least triple in volume. Cover and soak at room temperature overnight (about 12–16 hours).

00:00 - Drain and rinse the chickpeas, then carefully dry them in a salad spinner.
00:05 - In a food processor, combine chickpeas, herbs, scallions, garlic, cumin, coriander, and salt. Pulse until very finely minced; stop to scrape down the sides. Test: squeeze a handful into a ball— it should barely hold together (if not, process a bit more).
00:10 - Transfer mixture to a bowl, cover, and refrigerate for 15 minutes.
00:25 - Using a tablespoon measure, scoop heaping portions and gently shape into balls. (They won’t roll like cookie dough—this is fine.) Place on a clean plate.
00:30 - Pour oil into a deep skillet/Dutch oven to about 3/4 inch depth. Heat over high heat until it reaches 375°F (190°C).
00:35 - Carefully lower balls into the hot oil in batches, spacing them apart. Adjust burner to keep the oil between 350–375°F (175–190°C).
00:45 - Cook undisturbed until well browned on the bottom, then flip with a fork and cook until browned on the second side, about 4 minutes total.
00:49 - Transfer to a paper towel–lined plate and season lightly with salt. Repeat with remaining falafel.
00:55 - Serve immediately with tahini and/or hummus (and/or zhug).
